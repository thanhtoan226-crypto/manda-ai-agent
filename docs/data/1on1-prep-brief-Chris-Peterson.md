3. **Wednesday is by far the heaviest day (18.3 hrs across the month, 29% of weekly time in meetings)**

   His day-of-week distribution is uneven: Mon 7.4 hrs, Tue 16.2 hrs, Wed 18.3 hrs, Thu 15.6 hrs, Fri 4.5 hrs. Wednesday alone accounts for nearly 30% of his meeting time. Most meetings cluster between 10am-3pm, with a strong 10am peak (24.6% of all meeting time starts at 10am).

4. **High external meeting engagement (23.7%) across 16 unique companies**

   Chris spent 18.6 hours in meetings with external parties in April - primarily Google (6 meetings, 4.5 hrs), Atlassian (8 meetings, 4.2 hrs), and Searce (4 meetings, 3.7 hrs). This is above typical for an EM role and reflects active vendor management and partnership work (Google Workspace discovery, Atlassian advisory, Salesforce/Tableau renewals).

   You might ask: "You're carrying quite a lot of the external vendor relationships - Google, Atlassian, Searce. Is this the right distribution or should some of these be delegated as your team settles in?"
