### Recommendations

- **Keep: Weekly Sprint Sync** — high quality score (78%), strong attendance (92%), clear agenda with desired outcomes. This meeting is working well and the team relies on it for coordination.

- **Keep: 1-on-1: Direct Reports** — 100% occurrence rate, consistent 30-min format, high context clarity. These are your most effective recurring meetings.

- **Keep: Team Retrospective** — quality score 82%, full attendance, strong agenda usage (100%). One of the highest-value meetings in your portfolio.

- **Keep: Daily Standup** — short (15 min), focused, high response rate (88%). The 6-person format keeps it efficient. Consider switching to async on low-urgency days.

- **Merge: Tech Debt Review + Cross-team Sync** — 6 shared attendees and overlapping topics (both discuss technical priorities and dependencies). Combine into one 45-min "Technical Priorities Sync" — saves $1,920/mo and 4 hrs/month. - **Eliminate: 'Catch-up'** — 35% response rate, 0% agenda usage, declining attendance (-18% over 3 months). Costs $4,800/month for a 12-person group where most participants are passive listeners. Replace with a weekly async Slack summary.

- **Eliminate: 'Status update'** — 42% response rate, no desired outcomes tagged, quality score 28%. The information shared is already available in the project dashboard and Jira. Attending this meeting adds no value beyond what can be read in 2 minutes. Potential savings: $3,600/month.
