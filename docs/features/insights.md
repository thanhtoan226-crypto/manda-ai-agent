# Insights

Dashboards underneath, each with filters: Company, Country, Role, Team, Department, or specific employee.

## Time in Meetings

**Metrics:** Total Employee Cost, Total Employee Hours, Total Number of Meetings, % of Employee Time

**Charts:**
- Employee Hours Over Time — filters: metrics above, time frame (Day/Week/Month/Quarter/Year), grouped/stacked/none for Recurring vs Ad-hoc
- Employee Hours by Day, Employee Hours by Hour — filter: Employee cost, employee hours, number of meetings, % of employee time
- Pie: Recurring vs Ad-Hoc (Employee Hours)

## Meeting Effectiveness — Size & Adoption

**Metrics:** Large Meeting Cost, Large Meeting % of Meetings, Large Meeting % of Employee Time, Speedy Meeting Adoption, Response Rate

**Charts:**
- Large Meeting Number of Meetings Over Time — filters: metrics above, time frame, grouped/stacked/none for Recurring/Ad-hoc
- Number of Meetings by Meeting Size, Number of Meetings by Duration, Acceptance Rate by Meeting Size — filter: Total Cost, Number of Meetings (default), Employee Hours
- Table: Organisers by Company

## Meeting Effectiveness — Quality

**Metrics:** Quality Score, Agenda Usage, Desired Outcomes, Has Purpose, Context Clarity

**Charts:**
- Quality Score Over Time — filters: metrics above, time frame, grouped/stacked/none for Recurring/Ad-hoc
- All Preparation Metrics Over Time, Recurring vs Ad-Hoc Preparation Quality
- Table: Organisers by Company
